\section*{Exercises}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.6em]

\item A food bank is assembling care packages. Each package includes 4 cans of soup. If there are 23 cans available, what is the greatest number of full packages that can be made? Also state how many cans will be left over.

\item Manuel deposits 30\% of \$140 into a savings account. How much does he deposit?

\item Simplify
\[
\frac{\left(\frac{1}{x}\cdot \frac{y^{3}}{x}\right)}{x\,y^{2}\cdot \frac{y}{x^{2}}}.
\]
Then, evaluate for $x=125,\; y=36$

\item Write the fraction $\dfrac{9}{25}$ as a decimal.

\item Compute and simplify your answer:
\[
\frac{7}{10}+\frac{5}{100}.
\]

\item Express $0.08$ as an irreducible fraction.

\item Order the following numbers from least to greatest:
\[
0.205,\qquad \frac{11}{12},\qquad \frac{7}{3},\qquad 2.4.
\]

\item Order the following from least to greatest:
\[
0.12,\qquad 1.2\%,\qquad \frac{5}{8}.
\]

\item \(45\%\) of what number is \(36\)? Write and solve an equation.

\item A store stocked 180 cans of popcorn for a weekend sale. That weekend, 117 cans were sold. What percent of the stocked cans were sold?

\item Insert the correct symbol $(>,<,=)$ between each pair:
\[
\text{(a)}\;\frac{5}{6}\;\square\;\frac{7}{9}
\qquad
\text{(b)}\;\frac{4}{7}\;\square\;\frac{3}{5}
\qquad
\text{(c)}\;\frac{9}{10}\;\square\;0.89
\]

\item Carole works at a bookstore and a restaurant. In a 30-day period, Carole worked $\frac{1}{5}$ of the days at the bookstore and did not work $\frac{1}{10}$ of the days. On the remaining days Carole worked at the restaurant. How many days did she work at the restaurant?

\end{enumerate}

\section*{Solutions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.45em]

\item $\dfrac{23}{4}$ packages, that is, $5$ full packages with $3$ tablespoons left.

\item $0.30\times 140=\$42$.

\item Denominator: $x y^2\cdot \dfrac{y}{x^2}=\dfrac{x y^3}{x^2}=\dfrac{y^3}{x}$. Thus the whole fraction
\[
\frac{\frac{y^3}{x^2}}{\frac{y^3}{x}}=\frac{x}{x^2}=\frac{1}{x}.
\]
For $x=125$, value $=\dfrac{1}{125}=0.008$.

\item $\dfrac{9}{25}=\dfrac{9\cdot 4}{25\cdot 4}=\dfrac{36}{100}=0.36$.

\item $\dfrac{7}{10}+\dfrac{5}{100}=\dfrac{70}{100}+\dfrac{5}{100}=\dfrac{75}{100}=\dfrac{3}{4}=0.75$.

\item $0.08=\dfrac{8}{100}=\dfrac{2}{25}$.

\item Convert to decimals: $0.205$, $\dfrac{11}{12}\approx 0.916\overline{6}$, $\dfrac{7}{3}\approx 2.333\overline{3}$, $2.4$. Order:
\[
0.205<\frac{11}{12}<\frac{7}{3}<2.4.
\]

\item $1.2\%=0.012$, $0.12=0.12$, $\dfrac{5}{8}=0.625$. Order:
\[
1.2\%<0.12<\frac{5}{8}.
\]

\item Let $N$ be the number. $0.45N=36 \Rightarrow N=\dfrac{36}{0.45}=80$.

\item $\dfrac{117}{180}=\dfrac{13}{20}=0.65=65\%$.

\item (a) Cross-multiply: $5\cdot 9=45$ vs.\ $6\cdot 7=42 \Rightarrow \dfrac{5}{6}>\dfrac{7}{9}$.\\
(b) $4\cdot 5=20$ vs.\ $7\cdot 3=21 \Rightarrow \dfrac{4}{7}<\dfrac{3}{5}$.\\
(c) $\dfrac{9}{10}=0.9>0.89$.

\item Bookstore: $\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot 30=6$ days. Not working: $\dfrac{1}{10}\cdot 30=3$ days. Restaurant: $30-6-3=21$ days.

\end{enumerate}
