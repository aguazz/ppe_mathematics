\section*{Exercises}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.6em]

\item Find the equation of the line that crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,3)$ and passes through $(4,5)$. Give your answer in the form $y=mx+b$.

\item Simplify completely: $4(x+5)+4x+8$. Write in the form $ax+b$.

\item Compute $\displaystyle \frac{-6-(-9)}{8}$ and write the result as a reduced fraction.

\item Chris completed $48$ tasks in $16$ hours. Find the rate in tasks per hour.

\item Write $\sqrt{72}$ in simplest radical form.

\item Translate to algebra: “Five times as much as the sum of $r$ and $s$.”

\item Compute $\log_{5}(125)$.

\item A recipe uses $2$ cups of water for every $3$ cups of flour. If Jacoby used $8$ cups of flour, how many cups of water did he use?

\item Simplify $\displaystyle \frac{5x^{2}-3x}{x}+8$ (assume $x\neq 0$). Write in the form $ax+b$.

\item Solve for $x$: \(\displaystyle \frac{3}{11}-2x+\frac{2(x+1)}{4}=\frac{5}{4}\).

\item Solve for $x$: \(3x-7<8\). Express the solution set.

\item Water flows at $1.5$ gallons per minute. How long (in minutes) to fill a $150$-gallon tub?

\item A price increases from \(40\) to \(50\) euros. Find the percent increase.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\section*{Solutions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.45em]

\item Slope \(m=\dfrac{5-3}{4-0}=\tfrac12\); intercept \(b=3\). Thus \(y=\tfrac12 x+3\).

\item \(4(x+5)+4x+8=4x+20+4x+8=8x+28\).

\item \((-6-(-9))/8=(\,-6+9\,)/8=3/8\).

\item \(\dfrac{48}{16}=3\) tasks/hour.

\item \(\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36\cdot 2}=6\sqrt{2}\).

\item \(5(r+s)\) (equivalently \((r+s)\cdot 5\)).

\item Since \(5^{3}=125\), \(\log_{5}(125)=3\).

\item Ratio water:flour \(=2:3\). Water \(=\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot 8=\dfrac{16}{3}\) cups.

\item \(\dfrac{5x^{2}-3x}{x}+8=5x-3+8=5x+5\).

\item Multiply both sides by \(44\): \(34-66x=55\Rightarrow -66x=21\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{7}{22}\).

\item \(3x-7<8\Rightarrow 3x<15\Rightarrow x<5\).

\item \(t=\dfrac{150}{1.5}=100\) minutes.

\item Increase \(=50-40=10\). Percent increase \(=\dfrac{10}{40}=0.25=25\%\).

\end{enumerate}
